-- NSSF UGANDA FINANCIAL & ACCOUNTING DOMAIN GLOSSARY --
* Activity-Based Costing (ABC): Allocating costs based on activities that drive them.
* Actuals vs Budget (BVA): Comparison of real financial performance against planned budget allocations in the ERP.
* Accruals: Accounting entries for expenses incurred but not yet invoiced by the vendor at period end.
* Age Benefit: Benefit paid to contributing members who reach retirement age (55).
* Balance Sheet (BS): Report of the Fund's assets, liabilities, and equity at a specific date.
* Balanced Scorecard (BSC): Performance management framework linking KPIs to strategic objectives.
* Benefit Realisation: Measured financial and strategic outcomes from Fund initiatives.
* Budget Variance: Difference between actual financial performance and budgeted figures (Favorable vs Unfavorable).
* Burn Rate: Monthly rate at which an initiative or department consumes its allocated budget.
* CAPEX (Capital Expenditure): Funds used by the Fund to acquire, upgrade, or maintain physical and long-term assets.
* Cash Flow Analysis: Examination of cash inflows (collections, investment returns) and outflows (benefits, operations).
* Chart of Accounts (COA): Multi-segment organizational framework categorizing every transaction in the General Ledger.
* CIP: Cyber Insurance Policy.
* CIT: Corporate Income Tax.
* Cost Center (glmcu): Department, branch, or business unit where costs and operations are tracked (e.g. 999 = Head Office).
* Cost-to-Income Ratio: Operating costs relative to total income generated by the Fund.
* D&O: Directors & Officers Liability Insurance.
* Document Type (gldct): Transaction identifier (PV=Accounts Payable Voucher, JE=Journal Entry, RI=Invoice, PM=Payment, ##=Opening Balance).
* DST: Digital Service Tax.
* DV: Discharge Voucher for insurance or settlement claims.
* ECL (Expected Credit Loss): IFRS 9 bad debts provisioning for credit assets and receivables.
* EFRIS: Electronic Fiscal Receipting and Invoicing Solution (Uganda Revenue Authority).
* Encumbrance: Funds committed by purchase order/requisition that are reserved and no longer available for spending.
* Equities: Listed and unlisted stock investments held by the Fund.
* Fixed Assets / Fixed Income: Treasury bonds, corporate bonds, fixed deposits, and treasury bills yielding interest.
* GLA / GPA: Group Life Assurance / Group Personal Accident Insurance.
* General Ledger (GL): Central repository of accounting data (`staging.proddta_f0911_account_ledger`).
* IAR: Industrial All Risk Insurance.
* IFRS 9: Financial instruments accounting standard governing Expected Credit Loss (ECL) and debt provisions.
* IFRS 16: Leases accounting standard recognizing Right-of-Use (ROU) assets and lease liabilities.
* Invalidity Benefit: Benefit paid to a member with verified loss of earning capacity.
* JE: Journal Entry transaction in the General Ledger.
* KPI (Key Performance Indicator): Measurable target tracking Fund operations and financial performance.
* Member Fund Liability: Total financial obligation owed by NSSF Uganda to its contributing members.
* Midterm Benefit: Benefit accessible to members 45+ years old who have contributed for at least 10 years.
* Natural Account (globj): 6-digit JD Edwards object account classifying financial line items (e.g. 405014, 505102).
* OPEX (Operational Expenditure): Ongoing day-to-day operating expenses (salaries, utilities, maintenance, admin).
* PAYE: Pay As You Earn employee income tax.
* P&L / Profit and Loss Statement: Financial report of Fund performance showing revenues, expenses, and net surplus.
* PO / POP: Purchase Order / Proof of Payment.
* Posting Status (glpost): 'P' = Posted (audited/final transactions), blank = Unposted, 'D' = Deleted.
* PV: Accounts Payable Voucher document authorizing payment.
* Real Estate: Housing projects, commercial properties, and land developments owned by the Fund.
* Realised Income: Investment income received in cash or settled.
* Run Rate: Current monthly or quarterly performance extrapolated across a full financial year.
* Standard Contributions: Mandatory 15% monthly contribution (10% employer, 5% employee) under the NSSF Act.
* Survivor's Benefit: Benefit paid to designated dependents of a deceased contributing member.
* TB / Trial Balance: Bookkeeping report compiling all debit and credit ledger balances.
* TIN: Tax Identification Number (Uganda Revenue Authority).
* URA: Uganda Revenue Authority.
* Unrealised Income: Income from asset valuation changes (forex fluctuations, property revaluations, stock prices).
* VAT / WHT / WHVAT: Value Added Tax / Withholding Tax / Withholding VAT.
* Voluntary Contributions (Smart Life): Voluntary savings over and above mandatory standard contributions.
* Working Capital: Operating liquidity representing current assets minus current liabilities.
* Yield: Rate of return earned on fixed income investments and assets.
